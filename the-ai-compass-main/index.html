What the platform evaluates

Evidence for compute sovereignty decisions

The dashboard brings ownership, access, scale, and regional readiness into one filterable analytical model.

  • 01
    Country and organization concentration using HHI and CR4 measures
  • 02
    Open weights, training code, and unknown access conditions
  • 03
    Model scale, domain mix, growth, citations, and data coverage
  • 04
    Arab model participation, Arabic focus, and Egypt's global position
CONCENTRATIONMarket power becomes measurable

Dynamic HHI and CR4 measures replace a static impression with year, country, organization, and sector context.

OPENNESSAccess is separated from publicity

The model distinguishes open weights, training code, restricted access, closed systems, and records with unknown access.

SCALEGrowth is read with coverage

Parameter bands, median size, maximum size, and coverage indicators keep missing records visible during scale analysis.

REGIONALArab opportunity gets its own lens

Dedicated measures compare Arab output and global share, while a model table surfaces Arabic-focused work and Egypt's position.
